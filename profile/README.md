## WHO WE ARE

We're a group of friends who make games. The idea started years ago, around
2015. Along the way we cut our teeth on early
builds like Cloudfall and Undead Arena. Those stayed in the past. This org is
where the work lives now.
:
**members spin up their own projects and jump into each other's.** If it's
fun to build, it's fair game. Open worlds, multiplayer, singleplayer, whatever
the idea needs.

> **Status check:** nothing is released yet. Everything here is in
> development. We'll say so loudly when that changes.

## HOW WE WORK

| Rule | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Anyone can start something** | Got an idea? Spin up a repo and pitch it. |
| **Anyone can join something** | Every project is open to the crew. |
| **Finish what matters** | Lots of ideas start. The good ones get finished. |
| **Say where it really is** | Concept, prototype, in development or shipped. We label things honestly and don't hype what isn't built. |
